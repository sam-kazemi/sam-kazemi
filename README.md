# Hey, I'm Sam 👋

I study brains for a living; PhD student in Cognitive Neuroscience at
UIUC's Beckman Institute. My background is in electrical engineering,
so technically I've just moved from debugging circuits to debugging
the wetware that designed them.

## 🧠 The day job

* Predicting perception **before** it happens: decoding what people are about to perceive from their brain connectivity, in a 58-person simultaneous EEG-fMRI dataset where everyone does three tasks. SVMs and clustering, kept honest with stratified k-fold and leave-one-subject-out cross-validation plus permutation tests (no lucky splits allowed)
* Keeping two big **EEG-fMRI + behavior** studies running: I wrote and modified the behavior experiment code, worked out how to get all the devices talking to each other (then made it faster and smoother 😎), help run the recordings, and lead the EEG cleaning, which means training the RAs and making sure every file comes out right (EEG recorded inside an MRI scanner is very, very noisy)
* Using fMRI to figure out what executive function looks like in the brain — and why some brains handle change better than others
* Recording from sea slug neurons with hand-built electrodes for the Mind in Vitro team. The neurons are doing their best, and there's more coming on this one 👀
* Mentoring my first undergrad researcher through a **connectome-blueprint percept prediction**, and teaching students fMRI analysis as a TA
* Previously: an EEG brain-computer interface that let users control a cycling rehab system with their thoughts; pedaling, but make it telepathic (why not)
* Also previously: a year building PyQt and SQL software that kept a medical imaging device’s software and hardware in sync.
